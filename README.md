# Jacob_CTF
This is a CTF Challenge hoping to be finished by the time Jacob and I celebrate our 3rd anniversary!

# Setup
By the time this is out in the world, there should be a working website that will allow Jacob, and anyone else, to complete the CTFs fully online. Pending is the
name of that website. That's a future me decision. Right now though, download the project file in its entirety, unzip it, and click on index.html. This will allow
you to complete the CTFs locally. Progress should be saved automatically, but their is an option to back up your progress if you'd like.

# Usage
You'll be given a series of CTFs from multiple different categories. You can complete them in any order you'd like. The flags are themed off of Jacob and my 
adventures together since this is a gift for our anniversary. If you find a flag that is a bunch of nonsense, its probably wrong. You get as many attempts as you'd 
like, but you have to wait 30 seconds between each attempt (Jacob likes his scripts). Flags are not stored in the HTML information and you will not be able to find
them by investigating the infrastructure. As I think of other ways Jacob can abuse the system I'm making, even more limitations will be added. The final prize is 
locked behind a CTF that is only solvable after you solve all the other CTFs. When you complete that final CTF, you win, and you can consider yourself an expert of 
mine and Jacob's relationship.
